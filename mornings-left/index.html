Privacy Policy for Mornings Left

Developer: DKSoft

Package: com.dksoft.morningsleft

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Mornings Left does not collect or transmit personal information to DKSoft. The app works locally on your device without an account, advertising, analytics, or cloud services.

Information collection and sharing

Mornings Left does not require an account. It does not collect personal information, transmit user information to DKSoft, or share or sell user data.

The app contains no advertising or analytics and does not use third-party crash analytics, a backend or cloud service, cloud sync, or an advertising identifier.

Device access

Mornings Left does not request access to your location, contacts, system calendar, camera, or notification permission.

Local app data

Your selected workdays, work-start time, federal holiday selections, manual Working or Off overrides, and optional personal countdown goal are stored locally on your device. The Android home-screen widget reads the same local app configuration so it can show your countdown.

Android backup is disabled for Mornings Left, so the app does not ask Android to back up or transfer this local configuration to another device.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and the Android operating system may process information independently under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy describes the data practices of Mornings Left and DKSoft, not platform-level processing controlled by Google or your device manufacturer.

Children

Mornings Left is a general productivity utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

DKSoft may update this policy if Mornings Left functionality or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact DKSoft at support.dksoft@gmail.com.
